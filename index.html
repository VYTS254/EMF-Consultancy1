Understanding Your Tax Obligations

As an Airbnb host or short-term rental property owner in Kenya, you have specific tax obligations with the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). Many hosts are unaware of these requirements, which can lead to penalties and back-duty taxes.

Key Tax Obligations for Airbnb Hosts:

  • Income Tax on rental earnings
  • VAT registration and compliance (if applicable)
  • Withholding tax considerations
  • Proper record keeping and documentation
  • Monthly and annual tax returns

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Airbnb taxes in Kenya

Do I need to pay tax on my Airbnb income in Kenya?

Yes, all income earned from Airbnb or short-term rentals in Kenya is subject to income tax. You must register with KRA and file regular tax returns on your rental income.

What is the tax rate for Airbnb income?

Rental income is subject to income tax at progressive rates. The specific rate depends on your total annual income and whether you're operating as an individual or business entity. EMF Consultants can help determine your exact tax obligations.

Do I need to register for VAT?

VAT registration is mandatory if your annual turnover exceeds KES 5 million. Even below this threshold, voluntary registration may be beneficial. We can assess your situation and advise accordingly.

What records should I keep?

You should maintain detailed records of all rental income, expenses, booking confirmations, payment receipts, and any business-related costs. Proper documentation is crucial for tax compliance and potential audits.

What happens if I haven't been paying taxes?

Non-compliance can result in penalties, interest charges, and back-duty taxes. However, KRA offers voluntary disclosure programs. Contact us immediately to regularize your tax affairs and minimize penalties.
